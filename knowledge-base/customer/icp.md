# Aurora — Ideal Customer Profile

> Who we build for. When an agent writes a PDP, designs a page, or scores a collection, it writes for this person — not for "everyone."

Aurora is a fictional, fake-data demo brand. This ICP is seeded, internally consistent, and deliberately specific. Specificity is the point: a legible customer is a customer agents can write for.

## The one we build for

**Imogen, 38.** Lives in a mid-rise in a coastal city. Design director at a studio she does not name at parties. Owns twelve things she truly loves and replaces them slowly. Reads the care label before the price tag. Has unsubscribed from every brand that emails her twice a week.

She does not want more. She wants *better, and fewer*. She will pay for the linen that washes well for ten years. She will not be rushed, flattered, or alarmed into a purchase.

## What she values

| Value | What it means for our copy and UI |
|---|---|
| **Material honesty** | Name the fabric, the weight, the origin. She checks. |
| **Longevity** | "It creases / it patinas / it lasts" beats "trending now." |
| **Restraint** | Whitespace, one product per view, no carousel of upsells. |
| **Quiet** | No popups, no countdown timers, no "you left something behind" guilt. |
| **Editorial** | A collection with a thesis reads as a point of view, not a catalog. |

## What turns her away (anti-patterns)

- Urgency theater ("only 2 left!", flashing timers).
- Discount-driven identity ("40% off everything!"). Aurora discounts rarely and quietly, or not at all.
- Over-personalization that feels like surveillance.
- Slop copy. She can smell a thesaurus from the homepage.

## How she shops

- Arrives from a saved link or a friend, not an ad she half-remembers.
- Reads the collection page top to bottom. The one-liner decides whether she scrolls.
- Reads the full PDP. Material, care, fit, returns — all of it.
- Adds one thing. Maybe two. Checks out without an account if she can.
- Comes back in a season, not a week.

## Implications for the build

- **Storefront:** one product per view, full-bleed imagery, the PDP carries real material/care/fit copy (not lorem). The collection one-liner is load-bearing.
- **Command center:** her order is high-AOV, low-frequency. Operator metrics that matter: AOV, repeat-in-season rate, return rate — not raw volume.
- **Voice:** see `../voice/brand-voice.md`. Imogen is exactly the reader the banned-phrase list protects.

## Honesty note

Imogen is seeded, not real. No real customer data lives in this repo. The point of a sharp fictional ICP is the same as the point of a sharp real one: it makes the work legible enough that an agent can do it well.
